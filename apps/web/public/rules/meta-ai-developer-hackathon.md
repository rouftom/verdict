## About the hackathon

Apply to spend ten days building with Meta's newest models. Use the Muse Spark
series, Muse Wonder, Muse Image and SAM 3.1 through Meta Model API, run Meta
open-weights models, and code faster with the Muse Code coding agent.

Bring a problem you know firsthand, or an idea that feels out of reach. Build it
with the models, learn from the Meta team and partners in live workshops, and
compete for a share in the **$1,000,000** cash prize pool across two tracks: one
for indie developers and one for startups.

- Virtual, worldwide and free to enter if selected.
- Participation is limited to a maximum of **20,000 accepted applicants**.
- Selected participants receive **$150 in Meta Model API credits**, valid for a
  10-day development window.
- Teams: maximum 4 people; solo is allowed.
- One person submits for the team.

## Participant tracks

Every project enters exactly **one** track.

| Track | Audience | What judges look for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Indie Developer** | Individual builders and students, teams of up to 4 | Creativity, technical ambition, and whether it works |
| **Startup** | Teams and founders building a real product | Technical execution and whether it holds up in production |

A project submission may be entered in one track only.

## Project themes

Regardless of the track, projects must fit one of two problem statements.

### Build for Your World

Bring Meta's models into your domain. Solve a problem you know firsthand, free up
time and mental space, and make something possible that wasn't before.

### Push the Frontier

Use Meta's models to tackle challenges in software, robotics, manufacturing,
energy, materials and beyond. Give a machine a new capability, accelerate a
scientific experiment, or unlock a better way to understand and control a
physical system.

## Judging rubric

Hackathon project submissions are evaluated against the following criteria.
These criteria apply only to projects submitted during the hackathon, not to
applications.

| Category (weight) | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Impact (30%)** | What's the real-world potential here? Who benefits, and how much does it matter? Could this actually become something people use? Does it fit into one of the problem statements listed above? |
| **Use of Meta AI Tooling (25%)** | How creatively did this team use Meta's AI tooling? Did they go beyond a basic integration? Did they surface capabilities that surprised even us? |
| **Technical Depth & Execution (20%)** | Is the engineering sound and thoughtfully refined? Does this feel like something that was thought through with real craft? |
| **Demo (25%)** | Is this a working, impressive demo? Does the product hold up live? Is the demo video genuinely cool to watch? |

Weights sum to 100%. The judges' selections are final and binding.

## Submission requirements

- **New work only.** Everything presented must be built during the hackathon,
  Oct 26 to Nov 4.
- **Identify your contributions.** The demo must show only the features, code
  and functionality the team built during the event. Judges must be able to tell
  what is new; otherwise the project is disqualified.
- **Public repo.** The submitted repository must be public.
- **Demo video.** 3 minutes, required on the submission. It is one of the main
  things judges score.
- Submit through the Cerebral Valley portal:
  <https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon/hackathon/submit>
- Add every team member to the submission.

## Eligibility and disqualification

- Participants must be at least 18 years old and of legal age where they live.
- Employees, contractors, interns of Meta or Cerebral Valley (and their
  immediate families and households) may not participate.
- Banned projects are disqualified if they break legal, ethical or platform
  policies, or use code, data or assets the team does not have the rights to.
- Avoid generic projects, including: AI medical or health advice, basic RAG
  apps, simple image analyzers, "AI for education" chatbots, job application
  screeners, nutrition coaches, personality analyzers, sports analyzers or
  coaches.
- Duplicate applications, false identity information or spam are disqualified at
  the sponsor's sole discretion.

## Conduct

Participants agree to conduct themselves in a professional, respectful,
considerate, mindful and inclusive manner in all virtual spaces associated with
the hackathon, including chat platforms, video calls, forums and shared
repositories. Organizers may suspend, remove or disqualify any participant whose
behavior is disruptive, abusive, discriminatory, demeaning or otherwise
inconsistent with the spirit of the event.

## Notes for the evaluator

- "Use of Meta AI Tooling" is intentionally domain-specific in this example.
  The re-ranking app should read the criterion from the uploaded rubric rather
  than hard-code a provider name.

- The demo video is a first-class evidence source: it may show behaviour that is
  not obvious from the repository alone.
